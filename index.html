Clip Uploading

▶ YouTube · This app uses YouTube API Services — Privacy Policy · YouTube Terms of Service

Clip Uploading is a private publishing tool used by its owner to manage three short-form video brands: Streamer Bites, Founder Minutes and Market Bites.

It prepares short vertical clips from authorized source material (creator clipping campaigns) and, with the account owner's permission, uploads them to the owner's own YouTube channels and Instagram accounts and reads those channels' performance statistics to decide which kinds of clips work best.

The tool is not offered to the public. Only accounts owned by the operator are connected to it.
