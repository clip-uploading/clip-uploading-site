Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Clip Uploading ("the app") is a private tool operated by its owner to publish short videos to the owner's own social media accounts. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

Data the app accesses

  • YouTube (via YouTube API Services): basic channel information, permission to upload videos and set their titles, descriptions and privacy status, and the performance statistics (views, watch time, likes, comments, shares) of the videos uploaded to the connected channels.
  • Instagram (via the Instagram API): basic profile information, permission to publish content to the connected professional accounts, and insights about that content.

The app only connects accounts that belong to its operator. It does not access any other user's data.

How the data is used

  • To upload the operator's own short clips to the operator's own channels and accounts.
  • To read the performance of those clips in order to improve which clips are made next.

Data is not sold, rented or shared with third parties, and it is not used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

Access tokens and statistics are stored only on the operator's own computer. Statistics are kept while they are useful for analysis and can be deleted at any time; tokens are deleted when an account is disconnected.

Data deletion

To delete all data the app holds about a connected account, revoke access (below) and/or email lukasmechas3@gmail.com: stored tokens and statistics are deleted within 7 days. When access is revoked, the app can no longer read or upload anything and its local copy of the token is deleted.

Revoking access

Google account access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Instagram access can be removed in Instagram → Settings → Apps and websites.

YouTube

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
